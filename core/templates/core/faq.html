Get instant answers regarding Tax Regimes, GST Thresholds, and Filing Timelines.

The Old Tax Regime offers higher tax rates but allows exemptions and deductions under Sections 80C, 80D, HRA, and 24B. The New Tax Regime features lower slab rates with fewer deduction options, simplified for easier filing.

Standard documents include Form 16 (for salaried professionals), Bank Account Statements, Annual Information Statement (AIS/TIS), and investment receipts (80C, 80D). You can check our interactive Document Checklist for details.

Businesses selling goods with an annual turnover exceeding ₹40 Lakhs (₹20 Lakhs for Special Category States) or service providers with turnover exceeding ₹20 Lakhs must register for GST. E-commerce sellers and interstate sellers require GST regardless of turnover.

Filing a Belated Return under Section 234F attracts a fee up to ₹5,000 (reduced to ₹1,000 if total income is below ₹5 Lakhs), along with interest u/s 234A on any unpaid tax liabilities.
